### EP03 · Two startups — *one sails the clusters, one flies the drones*

**🛞 DrDroid** · YC W23 · Dec 2025 – now · Bangalore — *AI SRE platform: agents that investigate and fix incidents*
- Built the self-hosted platform enterprises run DrDroid on: Helm, Kubernetes, GitOps and DevSecOps, on-prem or any cloud.
- Lead the **VPC Agent**, the MCP layer that connects our agents to customer infrastructure without exposing it.
- Shipped **75+ integrations** (Grafana LGTM, Coralogix, SigNoz, New Relic…) the agents use to investigate.

**🛸 FlytBase** · Jun – Nov 2025 · Pune — *drone autonomy: fleets that fly with no pilot at the sticks*
- Right-sized Amazon EKS pod by pod and **cut the cloud bill by 35%**.
- Upgraded staging and production EKS with near-zero downtime.
- GitLab Runners on EKS with Karpenter: a fresh node per job, faster and cheaper pipelines.
- Grafana, Prometheus, Loki and Alloy; picked Istio after a service-mesh bake-off; tamed on-prem K3s + Kairos.
